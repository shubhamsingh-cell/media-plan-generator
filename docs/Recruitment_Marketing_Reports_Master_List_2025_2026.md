## Top 10 Trend Signals — Q4 2025 → H1 2026 (~500 words)

**1. "Low-hire, low-fire" is the operating reality of 2026.**
Across BLS JOLTS, Indeed Hiring Lab, ADP, SHRM, Manpower and Korn Ferry, the same phrase recurs. US job openings have held at ~6.9M (BLS March 2026), down from the 2021–22 peak but well above pre-pandemic. Hires (5.6M) and quits (3.2M) are both compressed — employers aren't firing, but they aren't backfilling either. This is the defining macro pattern of the year.

**2. The Appcast paradox: US recruitment costs ROSE despite a softer labour market — but UK costs FELL.**
Appcast's 10th-annual benchmark (Feb 2026, US-focused) found CPC and CPH rising in 2025 even with apply rates climbing to 5.19%, contradicting standard supply-demand theory. By contrast, Appcast's UK report (April 2026) found CPC/CPA stabilising or falling as UK unemployment rose to 5.2%. Two parallel labour markets diverged sharply.

**3. Apply rates are bifurcating along the "sitting-down / standing-up" axis.**
Appcast: tech apply rates hit **7.14%** while healthcare and construction lagged. This is the white-collar recession showing up in funnels — desk-bound workers compete for fewer roles while frontline shortages persist.

**4. Time-to-fill is improving on platforms but lengthening overall.**
Employ's 2026 report shows time-to-fill **dropped to 63.5 days** (from 67.7 days). Greenhouse's Hire Standard reports time-to-fill **+37%** at large scale. Bullhorn's top performers place in **<10 days** (and 22% in <3). Selection is sharpening, but volume is straining process.

**5. Salary budgets converged at 3–4% across Western markets — and the candidate-driven era is over.**
WTW projects US 3.4%, UK 3.6%; Mercer US merit 3.1%, total 3.4% (with actuals trailing projections); Aon US 4.0%. WTW shows orgs reporting attraction/retention issues plummeting from 53% (2023) to **24% (2026)** — a labour-power inflection.

**6. Voluntary turnover at decade-low ~10%.**
WTW, Aon and SHRM converge: US voluntary turnover dropped to ~10.1%. The phrase "Great Stay" (Appcast) and "job hugging" / "job chaining" (iCIMS) capture how workers are retreating from voluntary moves.

**7. India and APAC are the world's hiring engines.**
ManpowerGroup Q2 2026 NEO India **68%**, UAE 60%, Brazil 55%. Naukri JobSpeak hit a record 3,233 in February 2026 (+12% YoY). Aon India salary increases **9.1%** for 2026 — far above any developed market. Vietnam leads SEA at 7.1%.

**8. AI is no longer experimental — it's structural in postings and salaries.**
Lightcast/Stanford AI Index 2026: 2.5–2.6% of US postings mention AI (+55% YoY); Singapore 4.7%, Hong Kong 3.5%. Lightcast separately documented a **28%** salary premium for AI skills. Korn Ferry: **84%** of talent leaders plan AI in 2026; **52%** plan autonomous AI agents on teams.

**9. The healthcare workforce crisis is the most quantified — and most stable — pattern.**
NSI 2026: RN turnover 17.6%, vacancy 8.6%, time-to-fill 78 days, cost $60,090 per turnover. Travel nurses cost $66K more per FTE than employed RNs. AHA projects a 64,000-nurse shortfall by 2030.

**10. Sourced, referred, and rediscovered candidates radically outperform inbound.**
Gem 2026: sourced hires **8x more likely**, referrals **11x**, internal mobility **32x** more likely than inbound. **46%** of sourced hires now come from CRM/ATS rediscovery — up from 26% in 2021. Job boards drive 90% of applications but only 50% of hires. Relationship channels are where quality concentrates as recruiter teams shrink (-56% per Greenhouse) and applications swell (+412% per recruiter, Greenhouse).
